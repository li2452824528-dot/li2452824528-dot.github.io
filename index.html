Abelian Sandpile on Peano Fractal Boundaries

Simulation, structure, and computational experimentation

A computational study of Abelian Sandpile Models on square-grid graphs with recursively generated Peano-curve sink boundaries.

I implemented the boundary construction and stabilization routines, computed and verified identity configurations, and compared height patterns, odometer profiles, level sets, and toppling behavior through reproducible experiments.
