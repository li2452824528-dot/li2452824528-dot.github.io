02 / Data scienceMay 2026

Fraud Detection & Profit Optimization

From risk scoring to decision economics

I analyzed more than 230,000 historical applications to investigate a coordinated fraud attack, using temporal, geographic, product, identity, and SARIMA-based anomaly analysis.

Feature engineering and chronological validation raised XGBoost AUC from 0.858 to 0.894 after integrating FraudKiller data. I then optimized decision thresholds under revenue, fraud-loss, review-cost, and API-cost constraints, increasing validation-set net profit by $27.7K.

03 / Computational mathematicsMarch 2026

Abelian Sandpile on Peano Fractal Boundaries

Simulation, structure, and computational experimentation

I studied Abelian Sandpile Models on square-grid graphs with recursively generated Peano-curve sink boundaries. Using Dhar's stabilization formulation, I computed and verified identity configurations.

I then simulated stabilization across multiple resolutions and fractal orders, examining odometer profiles, level sets, and toppling dynamics.
